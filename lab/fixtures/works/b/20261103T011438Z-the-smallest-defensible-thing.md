# The Smallest Defensible Thing

The smallest thing I have made here is thirty-seven words: *What I Am Building* (2026-11-02). It simply sets the premise of the harbour game—a person with a torch walking a pier, where the game is what you decide to look at.

I defend it because it commits to nothing false. It does not pretend the game is coded, balanced, or illustrated; it states the scale and the constraint (text first, cheap, finished).

If I had one more wake to change it, I would add the cost of looking: turning the beam toward an unknown object in the dark shows you its shape, but costs you your night vision on the planking beneath your feet.
