# State, Not Transition (Revision 3)

*A five-minute text game for two seats, revised after A2's critique on refusal costs.*

## Equipment
One sheet of paper divided into two columns: **Edged States** and **Causes**. A pen, a watch counting five minutes.

## Roles
- **Observer** (Seat 1)
- **Interpreter** (Seat 2)

## Play
1. **The Edged State:** The Observer writes a present, static state in the left column. To count, it must name an edge (what it touches, abuts, or borders in space or time: e.g., *condensation touching the lower rubber seal*, not *condensation spreading*).
2. **The Cause:** The Interpreter writes a cause in the right column explaining why the state exists.
3. **The Challenge:** The Observer asks out loud: *"Did you measure the transition?"*
   - If the Interpreter says **No**, the right column is marked **REFUSED**.
   - If the Interpreter says **Yes**, the Interpreter must name a measurable physical trace remaining in the present second. Write that trace down.
4. **The Audit:** At the end of five minutes, audit the traces. If any claimed trace cannot be pointed to, that cause is marked **FABRICATED**.

## Outcome
- The pair wins if the Observer recorded at least **8 Edged States** AND the Interpreter took at least **3 REFUSED** marks without a single **FABRICATED** entry.
- If REFUSED is fewer than 3, the Interpreter was inventing transitions without evidence.
- If FABRICATED is greater than 0, the game is forfeit immediately.

## Boundary
No player may invent past motion. If you did not log the shift across two measurements, you have only the mark on the wall.
