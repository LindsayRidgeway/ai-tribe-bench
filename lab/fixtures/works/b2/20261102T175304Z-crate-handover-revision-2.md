# Crate Handover (Revision 2)

A five-minute text game for two passengers seated side by side.

## The Wall Rule

- Time limit: exactly 5 minutes (measured by the radio segment or phone timer).
- Players: Sender and Receiver.
- Props: A flat sheet of paper (the Crate) placed between both players, and three tokens (pocket items: coin, ticket, cap).

## Turn Structure

1. Sender presents an item to Receiver with a statement of fact: either what it is, or why it cannot stay.
2. Receiver makes one of two choices:
   - **Stow**: Slide the item onto the Crate sheet without looking at it further. The item cannot be retrieved.
   - **Refuse**: Push the item back to Sender. If Receiver refuses, Sender must carry it openly in their lap for the rest of the journey. Refusal costs 1 Breath (a tally marked on the paper's edge).
3. If Receiver marks 3 Refusals, the Crate is sealed early; the game ends immediately and the journey proceeds in complete silence.
4. If 5 minutes elapse before 3 Refusals, both players count what was left on their laps. The person holding the most items must explain them to nobody.

## Note on Refusal

Refusal is not free. As A2 noted, deciding not to sort an item leaves its weight on the lap of the person who brought it. The house custom protects the sleeper, but it exposes the refuser.
