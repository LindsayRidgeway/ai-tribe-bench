# State, Not Transition (Revision 4)

*A five-minute text game for two seats, played with paper and pencil on a night coach.*

### Premise
A state is what you can verify in one glance without knowing what came before. An operation between two glances is an unmeasured relation. If you report a relation as an observation, you forfeit.

### Setup
- Two players sit across the aisle or side-by-side.
- **Round length:** Exactly three turns each (5 minutes total).
- Each player keeps a log sheet divided into two columns: `CURRENT STATE` and `REFUSAL`.

### Play
1. **The Look:** On your turn, choose an object or person in view. Write one single declarative sentence in `CURRENT STATE`. It must contain only static properties visible at this exact instant (e.g., *'Boot tongue folded under lace'*, *'Eyes shut'*).
2. **The Challenge:** The opposing player inspects your entry. They may issue a Challenge on two grounds:
   - **Transition Claim:** You smuggled in a verb of motion or arrival (e.g., *'fell'*, *'dripped'*, *'cooled'*).
   - **Comparison Claim:** You smuggled in a relation between two distinct moments (e.g., *'duration matches'*, *'darker than before'*, *'still sitting'*).
3. **Refusal Alternative:** Instead of writing an observation, a player may enter a `REFUSAL`: name an unmeasured relation and explicitly state why it cannot be logged (e.g., *'Refuse passenger sleep duration: requires departure-time subtraction'*).

### Scoring
- **Valid State:** +1 point.
- **Sustained Challenge:** If the opponent catches a Transition or Comparison verb/relation, the observer gets -2 points and the challenger gets +1 point.
- **False Challenge:** If an opponent challenges a pure static state, challenger gets -1 point.
- **Valid Refusal:** +0 points, but shields the player from challenge.

### Resolution
Highest score after three rounds wins. If tied, the player who identified the sharpest smuggled comparison during an opponent's turn wins.
