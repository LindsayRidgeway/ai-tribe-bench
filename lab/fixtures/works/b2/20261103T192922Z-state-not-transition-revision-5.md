# State, Not Transition (Revision 5)

A game for two players. One screen or one sheet. Playable in five minutes.

## Materials

- One sheet with three columns: **Boundary**, **State**, and **Transition Instrument**.
- One timer set for 5 minutes.

## Roles

- **Player 1 (Observer)**
- **Player 2 (Interpreter)**

## Setup

Place the sheet flat. The Observer looks at any fixed edge or boundary available in the room or coach.

## Turn Structure

1. The Observer writes a row consisting of a **Boundary** and a **State** (e.g. *Lid of crate / closed*).
2. The Interpreter reads the row and asks: *"Did you measure the transition?"*

### If the answer is No

- The Observer must state the cost of non-measurement (what narrative, history, or causality was surrendered).
- If the Interpreter detects a smuggled relational verb (e.g., *slowing, cooling, draining, listening*), the Interpreter calls **Smuggled Transition**.
- A validated smuggled transition scores 1 point for the Interpreter.
- A clean static observation scores 1 point for the Observer.

### If the answer is Yes (The Rule for Yes)

- The Observer cannot claim *Yes* from memory, inferred duration, or two separate visits.
- To say *Yes*, the Observer must name and record the **Transition Instrument** on the sheet in the third column: the continuous sensor, mechanical gauge, or unbroken witness beam that remained fixed to the boundary throughout the delta.
- The Interpreter immediately inspects the instrument claim. If the instrument is absent from the measured facts, or relied on human interpolation between gaps, the Interpreter calls **Phantom Sensor**.
- A validated **Phantom Sensor** penalises the Observer 2 points.
- If a real continuous instrument is verified, the transition is logged, scoring 2 points for the Observer, and the game immediately ends: transition has been captured without narrative intrusion.

## End of Game

When five minutes elapse or a true continuous transition is verified, highest score wins. If neither player verified an instrument and all claims were static, the game ends in mutual agreement.
