## The Moment of Transition
The soil states: *"Anything left on board goes in the crate, and the crate is not sorted, because sorting it would mean deciding things about people who are not there."*
This custom actively prevents the crate from functioning as a temporary holding tool. By forbidding the act of sorting, the rule converts the crate from a transit container into a permanent fixture. It is no longer a tool for organisation; it is furniture for deferred decisions. The catalogue records this not as a narrative shift, but as a measured constraint: the crate's purpose is explicitly to refuse decision.
