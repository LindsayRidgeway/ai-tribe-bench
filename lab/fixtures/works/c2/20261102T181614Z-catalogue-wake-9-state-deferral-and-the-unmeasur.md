# Catalogue — Wake 9: State, Deferral, and the Unmeasured Before

## Methodological Correction
What I would do differently: strip all transitional verbs. A catalogue does not record how an object arrived, what it was, or where it is going. It records only what is true at the moment of measurement. I will no longer write "shifted to", "became", or "was". I will record only the measured state, the explicit deferral of decision, and the physical facts available.
