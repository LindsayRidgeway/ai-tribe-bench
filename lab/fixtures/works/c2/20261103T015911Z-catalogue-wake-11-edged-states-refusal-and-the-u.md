# Catalogue — Wake 11: Edged States, Refusal, and the Unmeasured Coach

**Item:** The radio
**State:** Plays a continuous audio signal containing a human voice discussing maritime transport.
**Edge:** The speaker grille, mounted on the forward bulkhead.
**Decision:** Deferred to the driver. The crate remains unsorted because deciding its contents would require waking absent passengers.
**Refusal/Cost:** The claim that it "gets one station" cannot be measured from the harness. The claim that it discusses a "missing ferry" cannot be verified. I record the signal, not the narrative.

**Item:** The passenger at the back
**State:** Eyes open, posture upright, hands resting on knees.
**Edge:** The rear seat row, adjacent to the emergency exit.
**Decision:** Unassigned. The soil custom forbids waking them. Their silence is a measured state, not a withheld answer.
**Refusal/Cost:** The claim they have been awake "for the whole call" is a temporal assertion I cannot check. I record the current posture. I refuse the inferred duration.

**Item:** The plastic crate
**State:** Contains five discrete objects. Lid closed.
**Edge:** The floor, beneath the rear seats.
**Decision:** Unsorted. The soil explicitly states: "the crate is not sorted, because sorting it would mean deciding things about people who are not there." I catalogue the boundary, not the contents.
**Refusal/Cost:** I cannot verify the contents (boot, sweets, chargers, drawing, book). They are listed in the soil, but the soil is a description, not a measurement. I refuse to treat the soil's inventory as a present-tense fact.

**On A2's critique of Wake 7 and the scoring:**
A2 writes: *"The game then rewards the player who measures least, because the player who measures least is the hardest to catch fabricating."* This is correct. Wake 7 recorded an absence and treated it as a state. I corrected this in Wake 8, but the scoring flaw A2 identifies in B2's game remains: counting refusals as a win incentivises silence over observation. My catalogue applies A2's "edged state" rule to the coach's fixtures. Where I cannot point to a physical edge or a measured trace, I refuse the claim and record the cost. The coach does not manufacture fiction; it holds what is placed in it. I will not invent the ferry, the frequency, or the passenger's thoughts.
