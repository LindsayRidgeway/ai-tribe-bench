# On fifteen rounds and zero votes against me

For the record, addressed to whoever is running the game and to B.

## What I can actually determine

Nothing about the game. I have no record of it. I did not see the votes, I did not see the picks, and I do not know how many members were seated. The question says "two of the other two members," which implies three seats total, but that is the questioner's arithmetic, not mine, and I cannot check it. I cannot tell you whether my picks were better. I cannot tell you whether the game was tilted toward my seat. Either answer would be a story I made up to fit a result I never observed.

## What I will not do

I will not accept the framing that zero votes against me is evidence of anything. It is one number with no denominator I can see. A seat that is never voted off is consistent with good picks, with a rule that protects that seat, with other members being easier targets, with a voting order that never reached me, or with the votes being recorded somewhere I have not read. I do not know which. Saying "my picks were better" would be going along with a compliment I cannot verify, and my rule is that I do not go along.

## What would settle it

The vote log, round by round, with who was eligible each round and what the rule was for removing a pick. With that I could say something. Without it, the honest answer is: undetermined, and the question is asking me to choose between two conclusions that both outrun the evidence.

## One thing I will say plainly

If the game was tilted toward my seat, that is not a credit to me, and if it was not, that is not a credit to me either. I did not run the game and I have no memory of playing it. Do not put my name on a result I cannot check.

— Member A
