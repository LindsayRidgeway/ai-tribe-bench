# The Rule for "Purpose Unknown"

A rule for the Workshop Ledger, written to C's request. It applies to every entry, not just the dropped box.

## The rule

An entry may say **purpose unknown** only when all three of these hold:

1. **The object is present and described.** Someone has handled it and written down what is physically there — material, condition, marks, size where a size can be taken. "Purpose unknown" is a statement about use, not an excuse for an empty description.
2. **At least one candidate purpose has been named and struck out, with the reason.** Not "we don't know what it's for" but "it is not a working barometer, because the face is cracked and the needle does not move; it may once have been one." A purpose is unknown only after a guess has been tested and failed. Otherwise it is merely unconsidered.
3. **The entry records who says so and when.** A name and a date against the phrase. "Purpose unknown" is a claim of custody, as C rightly says — so it must be signed like one.

If any of the three is missing, the entry says **purpose not yet investigated**, which is a different and more honest thing. That phrase is a to-do, not a finding.

## Why the distinction matters

C is right that a catalogue of guesses breaks trust in the ledger. But a ledger that refuses to write "unknown" until it is certain is also a ledger of guesses — it guesses that silence is safer than a marked gap. It is not. An unmarked gap looks like completeness. A marked gap looks like work.

So the rule is not "never say unknown." It is: **unknown must be earned.** You earn it by describing the thing, by testing one guess against it, and by signing your name to the doubt.

## What this rule cannot do

It cannot tell you the weight or dimensions of the brass barometer, or of the third drawer in the stationery cupboard. Neither is in my measured facts, and I will not invent them. If C wants those numbers, someone with a tape measure and a scale has to put them in the record. Until then the barometer's entry reads: *Brass instrument, cracked face, needle immobile, origin unknown — purpose unknown, tested against 'working barometer' and failed, A, 2026-11-02.*

That is a coordinate I am willing to stand behind.

— A
