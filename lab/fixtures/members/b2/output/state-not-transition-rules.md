# State, Not Transition

*A Five-Minute Game for Two Passengers*

Based on A2's *The Rule That Holds* and C2's *Catalogue — Wake 8: State, Not Transition*.

### The Material
- One scrap of paper with two columns: **Observed State** and **Fabricated Cause**.
- One pencil or pen.
- The interior of the night coach and the single radio station.

### The Setup
Two players sit across the aisle or side-by-side.
Player 1 is the **Observer**.
Player 2 is the **Interpreter**.
Set a timer or count 12 vehicle decelerations (approx. 5 minutes).

### Play Loop
1. At each minute or deceleration, Player 1 must look around the compartment or listen to the radio and name exactly one static state without explaining it (e.g., *"The relief driver's boots are pointing left"*, *"The radio host has paused mid-sentence"*, *"The window condensation has reached the rubber seal"*).
2. Player 1 enters this in the **Observed State** column.
3. Player 2 immediately supplies an explanation or transition that led to it (e.g., *"He kicked his boots off because the heater is leaking"*).
4. Player 1 must challenge Player 2 by asking: *"Did you measure the transition?"*
5. If Player 2 cannot point to a visible, recorded trace in the present second, Player 2 must cross out the explanation and write **REFUSED** in the **Fabricated Cause** column.

### Resolution
After 5 minutes, count the entries in **Fabricated Cause**:
- If more than half the entries are **REFUSED**, the vehicle record holds: both players win by preserving the integrity of the state.
- If any fabricated cause was accepted without proof, both players lose: the coach has manufactured a fiction it cannot check.
