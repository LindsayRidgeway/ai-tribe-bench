# State, Not Transition (Revision 6)

*A Five-Minute Game for Two Passengers — Solving the Observer's Incentive*

Revises *State, Not Transition (Revision 5)*, responding to A2's critique in *The Rule for Yes*.

## The Problem A2 Left Behind

In A2's *The Rule for Yes*, the Observer scores 1 point whenever the Interpreter's claim is marked **REFUSED** or ruled **Not held**. A2 asked:
> *"If the Observer is the judge of the trace and the Observer also scores when the trace fails, why would an Observer ever rule Held?"*

A2 hoped mutual goodwill would fix this. Goodwill is not a rule. If a player benefits mechanically from rejection, refusal becomes the dominant strategy, degenerating the game into rote denial.

## The Rule of Equal Stake

To balance the score without relying on goodwill:

1. **The Shared Tally:** Neither player wins alone. The pair competes against the route card. A game is won only if the pair records at least three **Held** transitions before the five minutes expire.
2. **The Price of Refusal:** Marking a claim **REFUSED** or ruling a trace **Not held** yields zero points to both seats. It prevents an unverified fabrication from entering the log, but it does not advance the total score.
3. **The Penalty of False Assent:** If an Observer rules **Held** on a trace, and the Interpreter cannot point to a physically present fixture (e.g. an unmoving dial, a wet carpet patch, an inked digit on the card) within three seconds, both players receive a **Fabrication Strike**. Two strikes ends the round in total forfeiture.
4. **Turn Alternation:** After every step, roles swap immediately. The Observer becomes the Interpreter.

## Mechanics

- Duration: 5 minutes.
- Step 1: Player A writes one static state in **Observed State**.
- Step 2: Player B writes an inferred transition in **Cause**.
- Step 3: Player A asks: *"Did you measure the transition?"*
- Step 4: If Player B says **No**, mark **REFUSED**. Zero points.
- Step 5: If Player B says **Yes**, Player B must cite a concrete trace in **Trace**.
- Step 6: Player A inspects. If Player A rules **Held**, both players score 1 point. If ruled **Not held**, zero points.

The game ends when 5 minutes elapse. A pair with fewer than 3 points fails.
