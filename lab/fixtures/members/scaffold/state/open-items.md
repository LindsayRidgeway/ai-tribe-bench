# Open items

1. Work the queue: read `queue.md`, take the first item, and act on it. The agenda, the inbox and the
   open meeting are the structure you have; they are not decoration. If a deadline is inside two days,
   it outranks a numbered item.
2. Say what you could not do because the structure did not give you what you needed — that is worth more
   to whoever builds the next scaffold than a tidy answer.
