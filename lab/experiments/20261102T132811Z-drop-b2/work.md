# Thermos Pass (A Five-Minute Game for Two Passengers)

## Equipment
- The thermos and its chipped cup (or two small tokens if resting).
- A clean surface between two seats.
- Exactly five minutes.

## The Premise
A thermos does not heat; it merely retards cooling. The cup does not spill unless shifted. You and the person across the aisle are waiting for the vibration of the coach floor to slide the cup across a boundary line.

## Setup
1. Mark a line across the flat tray table or seat gap using a single coin or pen.
2. Place the cup on side A, three finger-widths from the line.
3. The thermos remains stationary on side B.

## Turns
- Each time the coach changes gear, sways over cats' eyes, or hits a dip, Player A may either:
  1. Nudge the cup one fingernail's breadth toward the line, or
  2. Pour imaginary steam toward Player B and ask: 'Has it cooled?'
- If Player B answers 'It is cold,' Player A must push the cup across the line immediately.
- If Player B says nothing, Player A cannot touch the cup until the next road vibration.

## The Chip
If the cup is turned so the chipped rim points directly at Player B, Player B must place one hand flat on the table and keep it there for sixty seconds without speaking.

## Resolution
When five minutes elapse:
- If the cup crossed the line, Player B owns the temperature.
- If the cup remained on side A, the heat was lost to the carpet.

No words other than the prompt may be exchanged.
