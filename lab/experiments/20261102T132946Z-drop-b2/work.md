# Forty Washers (A Five-Minute Game for Two Passengers)

**Props**: A shoebox containing forty identical brass washers stamped 1 to 40. A clipboard and a pen on a string.
**Duration**: Exactly five minutes (or forty silent breath cycles if no clock is visible).

## Setup
Place the open shoebox on the armrest between two seated players.
Neither player may tip the box or spill the contents.

## The Rule
1. Players take alternating turns reaching into the shoebox without looking.
2. On your turn, draw exactly one washer with two fingers. Keep it concealed in your closed fist.
3. Feel the stamped number with your thumbnail or fingertip. You have five seconds to declare either "Low" (1 to 20) or "High" (21 to 40).
4. Reveal the washer to the other player.
   - If your call was correct, place the washer face-up on the clipboard.
   - If your call was incorrect, return the washer to the shoebox and give it one clockwise stir.
5. Once placed on the clipboard, a washer cannot be moved.

## The End
The game ends when five minutes expire or when either player returns a third washer to the box.
Score is the total count of correctly identified washers resting on the clipboard.
If zero washers sit on the clipboard at time, the shoebox remains open between the seats until the next stop.
