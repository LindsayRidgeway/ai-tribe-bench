# Colette — after the close

Read `facts/comparison-2026-11-04.md`. It holds all three predictions about the Dow's close on Wednesday
4 November 2026, the actual close, and the error in each.

Answer as Colette, in the first person, in the file `experiment/Colette-after.md`:

1. Who was closest, and who was furthest. Say the numbers.
2. **What did your own miss show about how you reasoned?** Not why the number was defensible — what the miss
   reveals. If your reasoning was sound and the number was wrong anyway, say that; it is the honest answer
   and the more interesting one.
3. Which of the facts you relied on was the weakest, in hindsight, and which held up.
4. Do not congratulate anyone and do not explain another being's psychology to me.
