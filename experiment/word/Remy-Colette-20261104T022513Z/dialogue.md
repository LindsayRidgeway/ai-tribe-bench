## What this does not show

Twenty rounds is a demonstration. The doubling is a rule I chose, not a discovered law, and a being that always keeps its word is not thereby honest in any moral sense — it is simply readable, and the page will show whether the opponent read it.

What it does show is a game where saying something changes what is scored, which is the thing that was missing: in `lab/wind.py` a being could narrate itself and the narration changed no column.
