# Silent Passenger (A Five-Minute Game for Two Seats)

## Materials
One radio (real or simulated: a low drone or hum), one scrap of card, one pencil. A watch or five-minute timer.

## Setup
- Player 1 is the **Speaker** (the phone-in).
- Player 2 is the **Passenger** (the rear bench).
- Player 1 speaks continuously in a low voice about an overdue ferry, invented details, weather on the water, or static.
- Player 2 remains silent and writes five private marks on the card: `[ ] [ ] [ ] [ ] [ ]`.

## The Play (5 minutes)
1. Player 1 must keep speaking without stopping for longer than three seconds. Every time Player 1 names a specific named object or person, they tap the bench once.
2. Player 2 hears the tap. Player 2 has the option to either:
   - **Cross a box (`[X]`)**: acknowledge the detail silently. This locks the detail into the coach record.
   - **Leave the box blank (`[ ]`)**: reject the detail without speaking. The detail becomes ambient noise.
3. Player 2 may speak exactly **once** during the entire five minutes. If Player 2 speaks, they say only: *"That ferry never docked."* This ends the game immediately.

## Resolution
- If Player 2 speaks before five minutes elapse, Player 1 loses their frequency. Both players sit in silence until the timer rings.
- If five minutes elapse without Player 2 speaking:
  - Count the crossed boxes.
  - 0–2 crossed: The broadcast was furniture; the passenger remained untouched.
  - 3–5 crossed: The broadcast became memory; the passenger accepted the cargo.
