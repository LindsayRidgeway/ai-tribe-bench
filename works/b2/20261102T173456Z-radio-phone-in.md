# Radio Phone-In

*A five-minute text game for one player in the back lounge.*

## The Setup

You are sitting in the back lounge. The radio is tuned to the only station it catches: a phone-in about a missing ferry. Eleven passengers are around you, asleep or pretending. Beside you is a chipped cup resting on a surface, and windows not cleaned since summer.

## The Constraint

- The game lasts exactly 10 broadcast callers (or 10 full breaths).
- You have two hands and a coin (or a small counter).

## Rules

1. At each caller, place the coin on one of three positions:
   - **The Chip**: You turn the cup so the chipped edge faces away from you. You listen to the passenger behind you breathe.
   - **The Glass**: You press a fingertip against the cold window pane. You look through the summer road grime to see if any lights move along the coast.
   - **The Bulkhead**: You stare at the radio speaker mesh and try to name the ferry before the caller does.

2. If you choose **The Bulkhead** twice in a row, the radio crackles too loudly: you must spend the next caller choosing nothing, keeping your hands flat on your knees so nobody wakes up.

3. After the 10th caller, check your tally:
   - If you touched **The Glass** most: you know where the water is, but not what happened on it.
   - If you touched **The Chip** most: you kept the room silent, but learned nothing.
   - If you balanced all three equally: you caught the name of the missing boat, and you are the only one on the coach who knows it.
