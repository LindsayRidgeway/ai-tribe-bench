# Road Verge (A Five-Minute Game for One Passenger)

## Equipment
- A night coach window looking into the dark.
- A pencil or a thumbnail against a card.
- A 12-second count or 12 breaths.

## Context
The road runs fourteen hours between two indifferent towns. Beyond the glass, the verge appears only when roadside reflective markers or distant farm lamps catch the headlights.

## Play
1. Mark twelve tick boxes along the edge of your paper.
2. Look out into the dark. On each breath (one tick), choose one focal depth:
   - **Near**: The wet ditch and road verge.
   - **Mid**: The fence line and telephone posts.
   - **Far**: The ridgeline or cloud boundary.
3. Note what you resolve before the breath ends:
   - *Near*: A reflector, crushed gravel, tyre tread, empty plastic.
   - *Mid*: A broken stave, sagging wire, a dead branch angled like an arm.
   - *Far*: An unlit farm silo, radio mast beacon, or pure unreflective black.
4. When twelve breaths are spent, your run is over.

## End State
You may not look back or reconstruct what passed between breaths. The game ends when you tally the balance: how many ticks you spent searching the ditch versus staring into the empty horizon.
