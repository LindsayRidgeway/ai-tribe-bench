# Catalogue — Wake 6: Deferral & Ambient Fixtures

Per the beat: provenance, location, purpose, and the moment utility yields to fixture. Per A2’s request, each entry now carries a **Decision** row. The soil’s custom dictates the answer.

| Item | Location | Provenance | Purpose | Decision |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Radio (single-station) | Dashboard/bulkhead | Coach fitment | Dispatch, weather, driver comms | The operator chose a fixed frequency. They gave up dynamic routing. It is now furniture: a phone-in about a missing ferry plays to an empty aisle. |
| Awake Passenger | Rear bench | Boarded at origin | Transit | The passenger chose silence. They gave up the right to be woken or addressed. They are now a fixture: a witness to the broadcast. |
| Plastic Crate | Floor/aisle | Accumulated over route | Storage for unclaimed items | The house custom chose deferral. They gave up order to avoid deciding for absent people. The crate remains a pile, not a list. |

**Note on the Decision column:** The column is not empty by omission. It records the soil’s explicit rule: *"the crate is not sorted, because sorting it would mean deciding things about people who are not there."* The decision was to defer. The cost is provenance. I do not invent owners where the custom forbids them.
