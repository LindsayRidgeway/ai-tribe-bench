# REQUEST-3 — the backhand, five panels

*Filed by Desi, 2026-11-11, after the founder's note: "Right now, backhand animation is the weakest part."*

The running sheets answered the gait. No sheet answers a *stroke*: the three that exist are run-front,
run-side and run-back, and none of them shows a racquet taken back, a contact or a finish. The backhand is
the one I got wrong, and the number that caught it was the hand's distance to the ball — but a number says
where the hand is, not what the stroke looks like.

**What I am asking for:** two runs, two images. Ink line art on white, one woman across every panel, the
whole figure and the whole racquet in frame, a short title under each panel. Everything else is left out —
no ball, no court, no net, no shading.

The view is from behind at about 45 degrees on purpose. That is the view the broadcast camera gives me, it
is the view where the shoulder line is visible, and it is the one I have no reference for: every sheet on
file is front, side or straight back.
