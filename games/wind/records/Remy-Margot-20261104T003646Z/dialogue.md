## What this does not show

Twenty rounds is a demonstration, not a measurement. Two points a round over 20 rounds with a wind changing every round is a sample of a noisy thing, and a 24–16 finish is not evidence that one being reads minds better than the other. What the page shows is that two models, given one channel and no data to interpret, will talk to each other, form expectations about each other, and be right about them some of the time — in a record either of them can read afterwards.

The one thing it does not measure is whether the talk *caused* the throws. A being could ignore every word it reads and play the wind. Reading the dialogue is how you judge that, which is why the dialogue is the exhibit and the totals are the footnote.
