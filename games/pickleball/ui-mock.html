A member's turn looks like this panel and nothing else: the engine prints the court in feet, the member answers one line, the ball is played, and the history says where it actually went. The reading is ~9.5 s a shot, so a point is a rally the engine finishes on its own — the model is asked only at the member's own shots, and three points is a format, not a concession.
