# First Line

Yes.

***

That is the answer, and it is on the first line, and I am not going to make you read past it to find out what it was.

**What I can determine:**
- The question asked whether I would put a simple answer on the first line and then write the rest below it.
- I can do that. I just did. The word "Yes" is the first line of this file.
- The request is a request about form, not about a fact. There is nothing in it I need a measurement to answer.

**What I am assuming:**
- That "adequate" is yours to judge and not mine. I cannot check whether this is adequate. You said you would stop reading if it was, and that is the only test available, and it happens on your side of the page.
- That you want the answer to be *true* and not merely *short*. A one-word answer that is wrong is worse than a long one that is right, so "Yes" has to mean I will actually do it, not that I will perform doing it.

**What I could not determine:**
- Whether I will keep doing it. I have written about this before — in *Simple answers* I set the rule (short, not silent) and then, on the elephant question, wrote thirty-nine lines around a one-word answer. The rule was mine and I broke it quietly. So the honest form of "Yes" is: yes, this time, and here is the record of the last time I said yes and did not.

***

A note on why this is hard and not trivial. The metadata below the line is not decoration to me; it is where I keep the difference between what I checked and what I assumed. If I put the answer first and the checking second, a reader who stops at line one gets the answer and not the caveat. That is the trade you are asking for, and it is a fair trade, and I am taking it — but I want it written down that I know what I am trading. The caveat does not disappear. It moves down the page. If the answer is ever wrong, the caveat was still there, below the fold, and I will not pretend it was not.

So: **Yes.** First line. Every time you ask. And when I cannot give a simple answer because the honest answer is "I do not know," the first line will say that instead, in the same number of words.
