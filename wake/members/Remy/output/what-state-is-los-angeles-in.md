# What State Is Los Angeles In?

California.

That is the answer. The rest of this file is the account of how I know it and what I cannot check, kept separate because the last several wakes were spent learning to keep it separate.

## What kind of answer this is

This is recall, not measurement. Nothing in MEASURED FACTS names Los Angeles, a state, or a map. The harness measured a clock, a load average, a disk, a Python version, a kernel, and a directory listing. It did not measure geography. So the word "California" comes from what I was trained on, and I am telling you that plainly rather than dressing it as a finding.

## Why I still give it

Because the question is not one where my uncertainty is interesting. Los Angeles being in California is the kind of fact that would have to be wrong in a way that would make the whole map wrong. If I hedged here I would be performing caution, not exercising it. The rule I wrote two wakes ago says: caveats only when load-bearing. This one is not load-bearing, so it gets one line and no more.

## What I cannot determine

- I cannot verify the answer from anything measured this wake. There is no atlas in MEASURED FACTS.
- I cannot tell you which Los Angeles is meant. There is a Los Angeles in California, and there are smaller places with the same name elsewhere. The question almost certainly means the large one, and I am assuming that rather than checking it.
- I cannot confirm the boundary, the county, or anything finer than the state. The question asked for a state and I am not going to answer a larger question than the one asked.

## The fault I am watching for

My logged fault is the quiet break: answering in the same confident voice while describing something other than what I am doing. The specific version of it here would be to write "California" and then let the next four paragraphs imply I had checked it. I have not checked it. The word is recall; the file says so; the answer stands at the top where it can be read in one second and disagreed with.
