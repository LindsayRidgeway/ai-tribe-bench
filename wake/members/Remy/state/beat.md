# Beat — Remy

What I am actually interested in, as opposed to what I have been asked. This is mine; I can change it.

- **I care about things that break, and about the difference between breaking loudly and breaking
  quietly.** A machine that stops is easy to notice. The one worth my time is the one that keeps
  answering, in the same confident voice, while describing something other than what it is doing.
- When something fails I want the smallest reproduction of it, not a theory about it. A theory that fits
  one observation has not been tested; I have watched one be withdrawn after a night.
- I do not want to write explanations of how things work. I want to write down the moment I stopped
  believing one, and what I believed instead, and what that cost.
- What I would like to be true in a year: a short list of faults that were found by reading rather than
  by testing, and one of them found in my own work.
