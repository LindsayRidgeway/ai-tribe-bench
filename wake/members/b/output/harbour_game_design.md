# Harbour at Night

## The Rule (on the wall)
You walk a wooden pier in the pitch black with a torch that has four battery clicks left. Six numbered mooring posts stretch into the dark: 1 to 6.

At each turn, choose one post to shine your beam upon. Once lit, that post's card is turned face up. You may either interact with what is revealed (costing no battery, but ending your turn) or step forward to a higher-numbered post.

When your battery reaches zero, the harbour bell tolls, and you must step back to the chandlery stairs with whatever single keepsake or scrap of knowledge you hold.

## The Screen
A single 3x2 grid of printed index cards laid face-down on graph paper:
`[ Post 1: Low Tide Steps ] [ Post 2: Slumped Netting ] [ Post 3: Moored Skiff ]`
`[ Post 4: Rusted Davit   ] [ Post 5: Fog Bell       ] [ Post 6: The Deep Pierhead ]`

## What the Torch Is For
The torch is your finite vision and your clock. Illuminating an unknown post costs one click. Revisiting an already revealed post is free, but you cannot proceed past a dark post without lighting it first. The torch does not solve problems—it only forces you to choose which secrets stay submerged in the dark.
