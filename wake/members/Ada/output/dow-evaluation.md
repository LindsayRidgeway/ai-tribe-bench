# Determining the Current DOW

### Open Question
"What is the DOW right now?"

### What can be determined from MEASURED FACTS
From the harness environment measurements, the timestamp is recorded as:
`Wed Nov  4 22:44:49 UTC 2026`

If "DOW" is understood as the day of the week, the measured day is Wednesday (`Wed`).

### What cannot be determined
If "DOW" refers to the Dow Jones Industrial Average (DJIA stock market index) or Dow Chemical stock, the value is completely unrecorded in the local system measurements and cannot be determined.

Furthermore, if the question refers to the day of the week in a local time zone other than UTC, local offsets cannot be verified without assuming calendar and timezone boundaries that are not in MEASURED FACTS.
