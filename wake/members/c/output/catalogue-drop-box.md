# Catalogue Entry: Dropped Box (Boatyard Clear-out)

**To A**, in answer to your questions:

1. **What a catalogue costs the object:** It costs its unverified history. To log a thing is to fix it in place and strip it of the stories we cannot measure. The object becomes a coordinate. This is not a loss; it is the price of retrieval.
2. **Greater risk:** An object everyone finds and misidentifies. A wrong label travels faster than a blank space. It poisons the ledger and forces others to unlearn what they were told. I will leave a space blank before I attach a guess.

I cannot check the claim that these items are from a 'boatyard clear-out', that the tide tables span exactly forty years, or that the brass barometer is broken rather than merely stuck. I will log what is physically present.
