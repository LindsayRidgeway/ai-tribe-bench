# Harbour Torch — Sample Run (Five-Minute Wall Play)

This is a recorded walkthrough under the rules established in `harbour-torch-rules.md` and `harbour-torch-sheet.md`.

## Constraints
- Clock starts at 12 ticks (each inspection takes 2 ticks; movement between posts takes 1 tick).
- Torch battery drains 1 tick per move or inspect.
- Six posts along the pier: 1 (Slipway), 2 (Mooring Ring), 3 (Davit), 4 (Locker), 5 (Bollard), 6 (Pierhead).
- Win condition: Reaching Post 6 before battery hits 0, recording at least three specific sightings.

## Turn Log

- **Tick 12 (Post 1: Slipway)**: Torch switched on. Light hits green scum on concrete and a broken oar wedged under timber. Decision: inspect the oar. Cost: 2 ticks. Battery remaining: 10.
  - *Recorded*: Cut mark on the paddle collar. Not rot.
- **Tick 10 -> Tick 9 (Move to Post 2)**: Step along wet planks. Dark water slaps against tires. Battery remaining: 9.
- **Tick 9 -> Tick 8 (Move to Post 3: Davit)**: Skip Post 2 entirely to preserve juice. Post 2 left in blackness. Reach Davit at Tick 8.
- **Tick 8 (Post 3: Davit)**: Inspect the winch drum. Cost: 2 ticks. Battery remaining: 6.
  - *Recorded*: Wire rope rusted through; fresh grease on the pawl.
- **Tick 6 -> Tick 5 (Move to Post 5: Bollard)**: Walk past Post 4 (Locker) without aiming beam. Sound of scraping below timber. Battery remaining: 5.
- **Tick 5 (Post 5: Bollard)**: Inspect the tie line. Cost: 2 ticks. Battery remaining: 3.
  - *Recorded*: Manila hawser trailing loose into water, cut clean with a blade.
- **Tick 3 -> Tick 2 (Move to Post 6: Pierhead)**: Final dash. Lantern bracket empty. Fog rolling across the channel. Battery remaining: 2.
- **Tick 2 (Post 6: Pierhead)**: Torch flickers down. Final inspection over the lip. Cost: 2 ticks. Battery remaining: 0.
  - *Recorded*: No buoy light. The radio phone-in from the coach is faint in the distance, or imagined.

## Verdict
- Posts inspected: 3 (Slipway, Davit, Bollard).
- Posts abandoned to dark: 3 (Mooring Ring, Locker, Pierhead lip details).
- Battery depleted exactly at arrival.
- Play time: 3 minutes 40 seconds.
