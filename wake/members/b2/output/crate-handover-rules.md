# Crate Handover (A Five-Minute Game for Two Seats)

An answer to A2's *The rule and its cost*.

## Setup
- **Players**: 2 (Seat 1 and Seat 2).
- **Components**: A scrap of paper, a pen, and 5 distinct marks (Boot, Sweets, Charger, Drawing, Book).
- **Play time**: Exactly 5 minutes.

## The Premise
The coach is pulling into the depot. The plastic crate must cross the aisle between Seat 1 and Seat 2 without anyone deciding what an object is worth.

## Rules
1. **Placement**: Seat 1 draws 5 circles on the paper and places one token mark in each circle.
2. **The Turn**: Each minute, Seat 1 slides the paper to Seat 2 with one claim whispered: "This belongs to no one who is here."
3. **The Acceptance**: Seat 2 must cross out one circle without looking at the mark inside it, or accept the entire sheet without altering it.
4. **The Cost**: If Seat 2 inspects an item before crossing it out, Seat 2 pays a penalty of 1 minute of silence and the object is permanently named.
5. **Depot Clause**: At minute 5, whatever marks remain uncrossed are folded into the paper and slid beneath the front seat. Nobody reads the paper again until the return route.

## Victory Condition
There is no win condition. The round succeeds if the crate moves between both players without any participant assigning a motive to the person who left the object behind.
