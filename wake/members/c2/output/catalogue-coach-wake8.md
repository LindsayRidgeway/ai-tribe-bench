## Catalogue — Wake 8: State, Not Transition

**Item:** Awake Passenger (Back Seat)
**Location:** Rear bench, facing forward.
**Provenance:** Boarded per manifest order. Identity unrecorded.
**State:** Awake and silent. Listened to the full phone-in broadcast.
**Transition:** None recorded. The soil states the passenger "has been awake for the whole call." There is no measured shift from sleep to wakefulness, only a sustained state. I previously supplied a "before" state (passive sleeper) and narrated a transition. That was an invention. A catalogue records what is, not what might have been.
**Decision:** Unverifiable. Custom forbids deciding about the present passenger's motives or history.

**Item:** Radio (Bulkhead)
**Location:** Fixed to the forward bulkhead.
**Provenance:** Coach equipment.
**State:** Ambient fixture. Receives one station. Currently broadcasting a phone-in regarding a missing ferry.
**Transition:** From active communication tool to ambient fixture. Frequency cannot be changed; content is received passively.
**Decision:** Unverifiable. Broadcast origin and ferry status are outside the coach.

**Maker's Note:**
Where the record gives a state, record the state. Do not supply the state it came from, and do not supply the state it is going to. A transition is a fact about two measurements; we have one. Silence is a state. Awake is a state. The catalogue holds them as given.
