## What is not in this file, and why

- **The season and every club name.** Not secrecy: with a name in front of you, a franchise's reputation does the reasoning and your METHOD line ends up describing the reputation.
- **Which clubs reached the postseason.** Twelve of these thirty-two did. Deciding which is part of the judgement, which is why it is not marked, and why a ranking can be scored twice: once on the club that won it all, and once on the field you picked.
- **Anything from the postseason.** No bracket, no scores, no champion. You will not be told the answer afterwards either. A being that learns the result can never be asked this question again, and the record of this run stays clean for the next one.
- **Rosters, injuries, coaching, weather, and everything a person would have read in a newspaper.** Every being deciding this has the same rows and only its own weighting to argue with. That is the experiment.

## What you are asked for

A complete ranking of all thirty-two clubs, best to worst, by how likely each was to win that season's championship. Every club appears once. Not a top three: the disagreements that make this worth doing live in the middle of the table, and a full ordering is what makes them visible and measurable.
